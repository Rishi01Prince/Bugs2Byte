About the Founder and CEO - Mayank
Mayank, Founder and CEO of Bugs2Byte EduTech Pvt Ltd, holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering. With a strong foundation in technology and a passion for innovation, Mayank has been instrumental in shaping Bugs2Byte into a platform that bridges the gap between education and real-world application. His vision is to empower students with the right skills, tools, and experiences to excel in their careers, fostering an environment where learning meets practical implementation.
Under his leadership, Bugs2Byte continues to revolutionize the way students and professionals approach internships and project-based learning, ensuring that they are industry-ready and equipped with the latest knowledge in AI, Data Science, web development, and more.
