About the Co-Founder  - P Rambhupal Reddy

With a deep understanding of technology and a strategic approach to business, Rambhupal has played a pivotal role in the growth and success of Bugs2Byte. His expertise in leadership and operations has enabled the company to provide cutting-edge educational experiences and foster strong industry connections.

Rambhupal’s dedication to empowering students through practical learning and real-world applications is at the heart of Bugs2Byte’s mission. His leadership continues to guide the company towards creating impactful opportunities for students and professionals in the tech-driven world.
